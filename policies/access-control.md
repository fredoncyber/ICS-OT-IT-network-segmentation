# OT/IT boundary access control policy — template

**Owner:** security governance and facility owner. **Review:** at least annually and on material change.

1. Deny new cross-zone traffic unless explicitly documented, justified, approved and reviewed.
2. Separate business, OT administrative, process, safety and third-party identities; enforce least privilege and accountable individual accounts.
3. Require MFA on supported remote access and privileged access mediation; document compensating controls where controllers cannot support it.
4. Permit vendor access only via approved broker/jump solution, session authorization, restricted time window, monitoring and revocation.
5. Prevent direct internet access to controllers. Document data export direction and application termination at IDMZ boundaries.
6. Keep emergency access break-glass accounts controlled, monitored and tested with safety approval.
7. Review authorization and flow necessity on staff changes, incidents and scheduled recertification.
