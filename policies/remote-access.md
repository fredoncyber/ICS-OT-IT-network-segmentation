# Remote OT access — template

Use brokered access through a managed IDMZ, with no standing direct route from enterprise clients to controller networks. Require approved tickets, strong individual authentication, MFA at the supported gateway, controlled jump host, least privilege, time-limited vendor access and session audit records. Apply endpoint health checks where supported without disrupting vendor support. Treat unattended modem, wireless, cellular and OEM dial-in connections as separate ingress paths to inventory and review. Maintain emergency support procedures approved by the facility owner.
